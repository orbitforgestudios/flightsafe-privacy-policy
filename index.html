Privacy Policy for FlightSafe

Know the Unknown

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Orbit Forge Studios ("we," "us," or "our") operates the FlightSafe mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle data.

Because FlightSafe is a tactical tool designed for security, aviation, and contested RF environments, operational security and data privacy are our highest priorities.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Location Data (GPS & Network)

  • Why we need it: The core function of the App is to detect GNSS (GPS) spoofing and jamming. To do this, the App requires access to precise location data (Foreground and Background).
  • How it is used: The App continuously analyzes your location, speed, altitude, and raw satellite telemetry to calculate integrity scores and detect anomalies.
  • Where it is stored: All location monitoring sessions and forensic logs are stored locally on your device in a private, encrypted database (using Room).
  • Do we transmit it? No. Orbit Forge Studios does not collect, transmit, upload, or sell your location history, tracking data, or session logs to our servers or any third-party servers.

Sensor Data (Accelerometer, Gyroscope, Compass)

  • Why we need it: To validate GPS movement against physical movement (Motion Validation & Heading Validation).
  • How it is used: Sensor data is processed entirely in memory in real time. It is not permanently stored or transmitted.

2. In-App Purchases & Billing

If you choose to purchase "FlightSafe Pro," the transaction is processed securely through the Google Play Billing Library.

  • We do not have access to your credit card information, name, or billing address.
  • Google Play provides us with an anonymous purchase token to verify that the Pro features should be unlocked on your device.

3. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

If you are using the free version of FlightSafe, we use Google AdMob to serve banner advertisements.

  • Google AdMob may collect and use data (such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data) to provide targeted advertisements.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through the advertising/privacy controls available in your Android device settings.
  • Note for Pro Users: Purchasing FlightSafe Pro entirely disables the AdMob SDK. No ad-related tracking or data collection occurs once the App is upgraded.

4. Permissions Explained

To function correctly, the App may request the following permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required to access GPS hardware and network location for cross-validation.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION: Required to continue monitoring and alerting you if the App is minimized.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION / BODY_SENSORS: Required to access inertial or activity-related sensor information used to detect potential spoofing conditions where GPS movement differs from physical movement.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to show the foreground service status and deliver high-risk threat alerts.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your session logs and forensic data are stored exclusively on your local device:

  • You have complete control over your data.
  • You can delete historical session data through the App's available data-management controls, by clearing the App's data in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

FlightSafe is a professional tactical tool and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
